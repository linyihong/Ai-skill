Companion to [`27-typography-layout-profile.md`](27-typography-layout-profile.md)、[`18-episode-vs-knowledge-accumulation.md`](18-episode-vs-knowledge-accumulation.md)。**候選 loop，不是 AI 直接改字級，也不是 workflow schema。**  
觀察：[`evidence/2026-09-22-layout-review-loop.md`](evidence/2026-09-22-layout-review-loop.md)。

AI 只產 review evidence＋`adjustment_candidates`；Mechanical engine 執行 render／驗證。分階段：mechanical_fit → cue_layout → visual_review → temporal_stability → scene_consistency → episode_review。FAIL 只重跑該層並標 responsible parameter。**Iteration budget**；撞 min／max 交 `contract_gap`／`data_insufficient`。舒服感不得直接寫 profile：Observation → candidate → independent verify → Learning Inbox → 跨集才 promotion。Phase 3 **不**改 workflow。
